# Hospitality-Booking-Analytics
An interactive Excel dashboard analyzing hotel booking trends, seasonal revenue variations, and cancellation impacts to optimize business strategy.

This repository contains an Excel-based analytics dashboard designed to monitor and analyze booking data, focusing on seasonal revenue and cancellation trends from 2015 to 2017.

## Project Overview & Insights

The dashboard investigates booking behaviors across different channels and lead times, offering the following business insights:
*   **Seasonality**: Summer months show significant revenue peaks alongside substantial losses due to high cancellation rates.
*   **Lead Time Impact**: Bookings made over 30 days in advance experience a 45.91% cancellation rate compared to 18.55% for short-term bookings.
*   **Channel Performance**: Online Travel Agents (OTAs) suffer the highest cancellation rate at 43.3%.
*   **Actionable Recommendations**: Business strategy can be optimized by providing alternative direct-booking options to reduce OTA reliance and seeking deposits to secure long-term bookings.

## Dataset & Tools

The analysis is based on the `Hotel Management Booking Analysis & Dashboard.xlsx` file. Key data features utilized in the modeling process include:
*   Booking dates, Arrival dates, Lead Time, and Length of Stay metrics.
*   Distribution Channels, Average Daily Rate (ADR), and calculated Revenue Loss.
*   The interactive dashboard interface was constructed utilizing Excel pivot charts and slicers for dynamic data exploration.
